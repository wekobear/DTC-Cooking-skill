## Claim Guardrails

List phrases to avoid or verify before publishing. Use exact policy language when available.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Required sections exist.
- Primary CTAs point to correct destinations.
- Product/SKU names and links are current.
- Images load and include alt text.
- JSON-LD parses when present.
- Mobile layout is readable and usable.
- Tracking hooks exist on primary actions.
- Risky or unsupported claims are absent.
- Remote assets and policy/product URLs return successful statuses.
- Add-to-cart, discount code, payment, confirmation email, and conversion pixels work.

## Open Questions

List the minimum questions needed to improve confidence, such as analytics access, current policy language, top traffic sources, paid landing pages, or inventory constraints.
